# LinkedIn Content Pack

Ready-to-post content for the RestoPanel company/founder page. Copy, tweak the
specifics (numbers, names, screenshots), and schedule. These are drafts you own —
LinkedIn posting itself has to be done from your account.

## Cadence (sustainable: 3×/week)

- **Mon — Value/POV:** a strong opinion or insight about the restaurant industry.
- **Wed — Product/feature:** show one thing RestoPanel does, with a screenshot or
  short clip.
- **Fri — Proof/story:** a customer win, a metric, a behind-the-scenes note.

Rotate a "founder POV" and a "quick tip" in whenever you have one. Always end with
a soft CTA or a question; reply to every comment in the first hour.

## Content principles

- One idea per post. Lead with the hook in the first line (it's all that shows).
- Show, don't tell — screenshots and 15–30s screen clips beat paragraphs.
- Talk to one restaurant owner, not "the market."
- CTA options: "What's your experience?", "Want a demo?", "Full guide in comments."
- Put links in the first comment, not the post body (reach).
