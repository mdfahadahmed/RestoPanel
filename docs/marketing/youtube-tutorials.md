# YouTube Tutorial Series

A "Getting Started with RestoPanel" series: short, task-focused screencasts that
double as onboarding and SEO. Each is 3–6 minutes. Record at 1080p+, show real
clicks, and link the relevant [docs](../README.md) in the description.

Production notes: script the first 10 seconds tightly (that's the hook + retention
cliff), keep one video = one task, end every video with the next one.

---

## Episode 1 — Set up your restaurant in 5 minutes

**Goal:** sign-up → live ordering site.
**Description:** "Go from zero to a live, branded ordering site. We register, add a
few products, set delivery and payment options, and place a test order."

**Script beats:**
1. Hook: "In the next 5 minutes your restaurant will be taking orders online."
2. Register at /register → show the dashboard + storefront appearing.
3. Settings: logo, address, hours, currency; enable delivery/pickup.
4. Add one category + one product with a photo, price, a variant and an extra.
5. Open the storefront `/r/<slug>`, add to cart, checkout with cash.
6. Watch the order land in the dashboard. "Next: managing orders like a pro."

## Episode 2 — Build a menu customers love

**Goal:** categories, products, variants, extras, availability, badges.
**Beats:** category ordering → product with gallery, discount, prep time,
ingredients → variants (sizes) & extras (add-ons) → Featured/Best-seller badges →
mark items unavailable when you sell out. CTA: "Now let's take orders."

## Episode 3 — Orders, kitchen and status updates

**Goal:** the order lifecycle end to end.
**Beats:** the orders list + filters → open an order → advance PENDING → CONFIRMED
→ PREPARING → READY → OUT_FOR_DELIVERY → DELIVERED → show the live customer
tracking page updating → print a kitchen ticket / KDS view.

## Episode 4 — Take payments (cash & online)

**Goal:** configure and demo payments.
**Beats:** payment settings (COD vs online) → place an online order → the secure
payment step → order auto-marked paid → where it shows in billing/analytics.
Note test mode vs live Stripe.

## Episode 5 — Win repeat customers (coupons, loyalty, reviews)

**Goal:** the retention toolkit.
**Beats:** create a percentage coupon with a usage limit → apply it at checkout →
loyalty points overview → moderate and publish a review → how they appear on the
storefront. Tie back to the blog post "Turn first-time diners into regulars."

## Episode 6 — Reservations & QR table ordering

**Goal:** dine-in features.
**Beats:** define tables + availability windows → the storefront reservation form →
the booking landing in the dashboard → generate a table QR that deep-links into
the menu.

## Episode 7 — Understand your numbers

**Goal:** analytics that matter.
**Beats:** revenue (paid orders), AOV, best sellers, category sales, new vs
returning customers, the daily/weekly/monthly series — and one decision you'd make
from each.

## Episode 8 — Go pro: staff, security & your own domain

**Goal:** scale and harden.
**Beats:** invite staff with roles → enable 2FA and passkeys, review login history
→ attach a custom domain → a quick look at the developer API + keys.

## Descriptions & SEO

- Title formula: "How to [task] in RestoPanel" or "[Task] — RestoPanel Tutorial".
- First two description lines: the outcome + a timestamped chapter list.
- Always include: link to /register, the matching guide in /docs, and the next
  episode.
- Add chapters (timestamps) — they rank as key moments and lift retention.
- Thumbnails: one big verb ("SET UP", "GET PAID"), a product screenshot, the dark
  brand background (#0a0a0c) with the gold/violet accent.

## Repurposing

Every episode → a 30–60s vertical clip (the single best moment) for Shorts,
LinkedIn and Instagram, plus a written how-to that can become a Help Center or
blog article. Record once, publish five times.
